# v8 / v8b: Chakkhu's data, and a fair KHOB (2026-11-12)

Goal: beat Chakkhu-OCR-Khmer (Apache-2.0, 39 MB int8; see `weight_space.md`) using the data it trains on.

## Data

| Source | Licence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Chanrith123333/khmer_english_ocr_image_line | CC BY 4.0 | in use (registry `mrrtmob`, the same set); 100k lines from v8 |
| SoyVitou/62k-images-khmer-printed-dataset | MIT | in use (`soyvitou`) |
| Sokheng/khmer-synthetic-ocr-v1-100k | CC BY 4.0 | in use (`sokheng`) |
| Thareah/khmer-handwriting | MIT | new in v8 (`thareah`; its test split `thareah_test` is eval only) |
| Khmer Wikipedia (wikimedia/wikipedia 20231101.km) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | new in v8: text for `synth_doc` (`render_synth.py --doc --wiki_km`) |
| Open Development Cambodia laws and decrees | per document | new in v8b (`odc`): 238 scanned PDFs, 25,427 lines cut by the app's own line splitter, labelled by Chakkhu; 14,901 lines kept at conf >= 0.97 (`scripts/odc_pseudo.py`; `odc_test`, 908 lines from other documents, eval only) |
| Google Fonts | OFL | in use (`fetch_fonts.py`) |
| KHOB (EKYCSolutions) | MIT | test only, never trained on |

`synth_doc` (120k lines) renders three lines at scan size (12-30 px tall bands) and crops the middle one with
slivers of its neighbours, then a scan look (blur, noise, JPEG) or a phone look (light ramp, colour cast).

- v8: from M2 (`v7b_last`), `scripts/run_v8.sh`, 24,478 steps at batch 32 on the 3090.
- v8b: from v8, `scripts/run_v8b.sh`: `odc` 30k (2 epochs) + `synth_doc` 30k + a third of v8's mix, 7,030 steps.

## KHOB boxes drift on the scanned pages

The task-3 and task-4 annotations are about 2% too tall against their images: right at the top of a page, most
of a line low at the bottom. A crop then shows the line below its label, and a model that reads it correctly
scores ~100% CER on that line. `build_khob_fixtures.py` now fits `y' = a*y + b` per page so box middles land on
ink and box edges on the gaps between lines (`fixtures_khob_reg`). Fits: tasks 1-2 a = 1.00 +- 0.005, b within
+-4 px (unchanged); task 3 a = 0.96-0.985; task 4 a = 0.97-1.00. Task-2 goc_3/goc_4 (images of another letter)
stay excluded.

## Own sets

Mean CER. Pipeline = training/eval `trocr-pipeline` (PyTorch, app preprocessing) on the 3090; Mac = CoreML.
"Folded" counts ្ដ and ្ត as one letter: they look the same in most fonts, every label set uses both
(KHOB 180 vs 968, ODC 26 vs 2,137), and v8 / v8b write ្ត more often than M2.

Frozen on the Mac differs in 3 characters over 200 lines (a space, ្ដ -> ្ត, one ")"). handwriting_test moves
on 14 of 307 lines, both ways. odc_test labels are Chakkhu's, so 2.1% means "reads these scans like Chakkhu",
not an independent accuracy.

Desktop int8 as shipped (CPU, `trocr-desktop`, mean CER, M2 -> v8b): frozen 0.44 -> 0.56, screen 1.58 -> 1.52,
slices 1.06 -> 1.01, noisy 5.86 -> 4.96. Frozen differs on 5 of 200 lines: the Mac's three plus one space and
"flying" read as "Jijing" (M2: "flyving"). Median time per image unchanged (1.25 -> 1.33 s on frozen, others +-2%).
